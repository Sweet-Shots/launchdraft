FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need to know anything technical?

Not a thing. You describe your business, and we handle the design, build, hosting, and every edit after launch.

Who owns the domain?

You do. The custom domain represents your business and stays yours. We just set it up and keep it running.

What if I already bought a domain?

Perfect, we'll use it. Just point it our way and we'll connect it to your new site. We send simple instructions, or we can handle the setup for you. Since you already have it, there's no domain cost on your plan.

What does the setup fee cover?

The one-time design and build: copywriting, layout, your pages, and getting everything live. The monthly fee then covers hosting and ongoing edits.

Can I upgrade later?

Absolutely. Start on Standard and move up to Professional as you grow. Your site comes with you.
