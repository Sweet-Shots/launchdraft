The process

Three steps from idea to online.

No jargon, no drawn-out builds, no surprise invoices. Here's exactly how your site comes together.

01

Draft

Tell us about your business in a short call or form. We write the copy and design a first draft you can actually look at — usually within a few days.

02

Review

You give feedback in plain English. We refine colors, wording, and photos until it feels like you — no design software required.

03

Launch

We connect your free custom domain, secure it, and put it live. From then on, edits are a message away and handled by us.

Questions

The things people ask before they start.

Do I need to know anything technical?

Not a thing. You describe your business, we handle design, build, hosting, and every edit after launch.

Who owns the domain?

You do. The custom domain represents your business and stays yours. We just set it up and keep it running.

What does the setup fee cover?

The one-time design and build — copywriting, layout, your pages, and getting everything live. The monthly fee covers hosting and ongoing edits.

Absolutely. Start on Standard and move to Professional or Enterprise as you grow — your site comes with you.
