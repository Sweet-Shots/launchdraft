1.What we provide

LaunchDraft designs, builds, hosts, and maintains websites for local businesses. The details of your build — pages, features, and price — are set out in the plan you choose and the agreement you sign before we start the full build.

2.The free draft

We may build you a free homepage draft so you can see what your site could look like before you pay anything. The draft is offered at no cost and with no obligation. The draft and its code remain LaunchDraft's property until your setup fee is paid.

3.Plans, billing & auto-renewal

Our plans have two parts: a one-time setup fee and a monthly fee that covers hosting, security, upkeep, and your monthly edits.

  • The setup fee is paid before we begin the full build.
  • The monthly fee begins when your site goes live.
  • Your monthly plan renews automatically each month and your payment method is charged until you cancel. When you sign up, you agree to this recurring charge; the exact amount and billing date are shown at checkout and in your signed agreement.
  • We send you a reminder before each renewal so a charge is never a surprise.
  • Prices for your plan won't change without us telling you in advance.

Auto-renewal, in plain terms: the monthly fee keeps you online and looked after, so it repeats every month on its own until you stop it. You can cancel any time (see below) — cancelling is as easy as signing up.

4.Cancelling

  • You can cancel your monthly plan at any time — by email, by replying to any billing message, or however you first reached us. No phone calls, no hoops, and never harder than signing up was.
  • Hosting and edits continue until the end of your current paid month, then stop, and the live site comes offline. On request, we'll send you an export of your site's files so you keep your work.
  • The one-time setup fee covers work already done and is not refundable once the build has started. Monthly fees already paid are not refunded for partial months.
  • Your domain stays yours — we will transfer it to you on request so you keep your web address.

5.Domains

A custom domain is included with your plan. We register and manage it for you as part of your monthly fee, so you never get a separate domain bill. If you leave, we will transfer the domain to a registrar account you control, on request, and you would then take over its small yearly renewal. Note that domains can't be transferred within 60 days of being registered or renewed — a standard rule set by the internet's domain system, not by us.

6.Who owns what

  • Your content is yours — the text, logo, and photos you provide.
  • Your site becomes yours once paid — after your setup fee is paid, you own the design and content of your launched site. The live, hosted version stays online while your plan is active, and if you cancel we can send you an export of your site's files so you keep your work.
  • Any reusable templates, code, and tools we build our business on remain ours.
